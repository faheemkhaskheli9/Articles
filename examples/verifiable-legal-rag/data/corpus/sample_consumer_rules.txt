Title: Sample Consumer Protection Rules (Illustrative)
Notice: Original fictional text written for this tutorial. It is not a real
Pakistani law, does not paraphrase one, and is not legal advice.

Rule 1. Refund for Defective Goods
A consumer who receives defective goods may request a refund or replacement
within 14 days of delivery, on presentation of proof of purchase.

Rule 2. Price Display
A seller shall display the retail price of goods inclusive of all taxes.

Rule 3. Warranty Repairs
Where a seller provides a written warranty, a repair claim made within the
warranty period shall be completed within 15 working days.

Rule 4. Complaint Procedure
A consumer may file a written complaint with the Consumer Forum within 30 days
after the seller refuses a refund or repair.
