Title: Sample Tenancy Act (Illustrative)
Notice: Original fictional text written for this tutorial. It is not a real
Pakistani statute, does not paraphrase one, and is not legal advice.

Section 1. Scope
This illustrative Act applies to written tenancy agreements for residential
premises in the fictional District of Example.

Section 2. Security Deposit
A landlord shall not demand a security deposit exceeding three months of rent.
The security deposit shall be returned within 30 days after the tenant vacates
the premises, less any amount deducted for unpaid rent or damage beyond normal
wear and tear.

Section 3. Rent Increase
Rent may be increased only once in any period of twelve months. The landlord
shall give the tenant written notice at least 60 days before the increase
takes effect.

Section 4. Notice to Vacate
A landlord seeking possession of the premises shall serve the tenant a written
notice to vacate of not less than 90 days, stating the ground on which
possession is sought.

Section 5. Repairs
The landlord is responsible for structural repairs and for keeping water and
electricity connections in working order. The tenant is responsible for minor
repairs arising from ordinary daily use.

Section 6. Subletting
A tenant shall not sublet the whole or any part of the premises without the
prior written consent of the landlord.

Section 7. Eviction Without Order Prohibited
No landlord shall remove a tenant, cut off water or electricity, or change the
locks to compel the tenant to leave, except under an order of the Rent
Tribunal.

Section 8. Rent Receipts
The landlord shall issue a signed receipt for every payment of rent received
in cash.
